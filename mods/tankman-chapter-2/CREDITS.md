Vs. Tankman Chapter 2 PLUS is an unofficial fan mod.

Friday Night Funkin', Tankman, Boyfriend and Girlfriend belong to their
respective creators. Character sprites are reused from the engine's base
game assets rather than redrawn.

Redeploy, Blackout, Onslaught, Warzone, Last Stand and No More Heroes:
original synthesized experimental Chapter 2 arrangements.

Ugh: original Friday Night Funkin' composition by Kawai Sprite; this fan
remix follows the MIDI supplied by Senhor Laranja. The MIDI transcription
author was not provided. New radio instrumentation and playable charts
were rendered for this mod.

BF and Tankman vocal takes derive from the user-supplied DirectWave voice
addon. Raw DWP banks and embedded private paths are not distributed.
